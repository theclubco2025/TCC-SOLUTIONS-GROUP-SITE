About

Our mission

TCC Solutions Group LLC exists to take repetitive work off a small business’s plate and hand back software its people can trust. We work with owner-operated and mid-sized businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets and disconnected tools. We scope the work in plain language, build it ourselves, and stay responsible for what we ship.

Repetitive work

Quotes retyped into invoices, bookings copied between calendars, reminders sent by hand. This is the kind of work software is built to hold.

Disconnected tools

Most businesses run on several tools that were never designed to talk to each other. We connect them, or replace the gap with something purpose-built.

Hard-to-reach information

Numbers that live in exports and inboxes are hard to act on. We put the ones that matter somewhere they can be read at a glance.

The process

Three steps, scoped in plain language

No massive overhauls and no year-long transformations. We agree what is being built, build it, and stay involved once it is running.

1

Audit & discover

We walk through how the business runs today and identify the manual steps, gaps in follow-up and reporting blind spots worth addressing first.

2

Design & build

We agree the scope in writing, then build it — AI assistant, automation, web app or site — around how your business actually works.

3

Launch & support

We deploy the system, show your team how to run it, and stay available to adjust it as the business changes.
