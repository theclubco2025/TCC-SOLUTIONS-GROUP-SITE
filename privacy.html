2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. If you email us, call us, or book a call, we receive what you choose to send: your name, email address, phone number, company name, and anything you write to us.
  • Booking information. Calls on this site are booked through Calendly. When you book, Calendly collects your details and shares them with us so we can hold the meeting.
  • Technical information. Our hosting provider records standard server logs, including IP address, browser type, pages requested and timestamps. We use these to keep the site running and secure.

This site does not host its own contact form, does not require an account, and does not run advertising or cross-site tracking.

3. How we use information

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange or hold a call.
  • To provide, scope, quote for and support work you have asked us about or engaged us for.
  • To operate, secure and improve this website.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use the information you send us to build advertising profiles.

4. Messaging and SMS

We may send text messages only to people who have given us their mobile number and agreed to receive them, or where messaging is part of a service you have asked us to run. Messages relate to the enquiry, appointment or project at hand.

  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to any message to opt out. Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the details above.

  • We do not sell, rent or share mobile phone numbers, opt-in information or consent with third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Numbers are shared only with the messaging providers that deliver messages on our behalf.
